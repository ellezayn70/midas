The honest story: the first design was a 1-second high-frequency market maker — the classic MM dream. It doesn't survive contact with an LLM tick engine; the brain needs minutes, not milliseconds. So MIDAS was rebuilt as slow, wide, and safe: 10-minute cadence, 0.10%+ spreads per side, delta-neutral so wide is safe. Fewer fills — every fill positive-EV — and a bot that's still standing at hour 47.

Implementation

Detect. Hedge. Size.

Adverse Selection Detection

  • Five reads: OBI · VPIN · size z-score · cancel rate · book tilt
  • P(informed) ≥ 0.70
  • Every quote on that pair is cancelled. Immediately.
  • MIDAS stands aside until the book is clean

Dynamic Hedging

  • Δ = spot inventory + perp inventory
  • Spot LONG ↔ perp SHORT
  • Hedge on the same tick it is issued
  • A dump cannot eat the spread just collected
  • Self-heal: if a hedge slips, MIDAS re-checks the book and closes the gap on the next tick — no stuck long

Position Sizing

  • Max $100 notional per side · 1× on both books
  • Stop-loss 2% / take-profit 2%
  • 1-hour time limit / trailing 1.5%
  • Self-correcting: re-reads the book every tick
