Who's behind the book?

I've been running Hummingbot Pure Market Making v1 since late 2022. Primarily because my Python coding is non-existent.

Then Condor happened — the actual pivot. For the first time I could ship an agent that thinks, without becoming an engineer first. The strategy could live in English. That's when MIDAS stopped being a sketch and became something I could actually run: a market maker that reads both books, hedges itself flat, and steps aside when the flow is informed.

Why Bitget

I went looking for one house that could hold the whole idea. Most venues give you perps or gold — not both, and not on the same names as spot.

Spot + perps, same pairs. BTC, ETH, SOL on both books — so a hybrid maker can actually post twice.

A live gold perp. XAU-USDT runs 24/7. Traditional gold sleeps; this book doesn't. Extra tape, extra fills.

Depth that can take a quote. Futures tape thick enough that dual-book MM isn't shouting into an empty room. That's why MIDAS trades here.

Risk

Three layers. No exceptions.

I · The shield outranks everything

P(informed) ≥ 0.70 → all quotes on that pair are cancelled, immediately. No committee, no appeal. Re-evaluate next tick.

II · Delta-neutral is the point

Spot LONG ↔ perp SHORT. Net delta per pair stays inside the cap; hedge verdicts are acted on the same tick they're issued.

III · The platform won't let it be reckless

Max $100 notional per side · 1x on spot and perps (so a hedge actually cancels) · stop-loss 3% / take-profit 3% / 3-hour time limit / trailing 1.5% on every position — enforced, not suggested.
